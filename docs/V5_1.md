# Pocket Drop v5.1: the open review items, fixed and redeployed

v5.1 is v5 with the remaining review items fixed in the contracts, redeployed on Diana testnet on 26 Nov 2026
(about 02:30 Cairo time). New Pockets, drops and pools use v5.1. Links and Pockets made on v5 and v4 stay on those contracts
and keep working (claim, refund, cancel), so no old link breaks.

## What changed

| Item | v5 | v5.1 |
|---|---|---|
| F1 delivery fee on the first bind | paid to whoever submitted the bind, so a copied bind collected `relayerFee` | the link key signs `feeTo` inside the bind digest (`POCKETDROP_BIND_V3`); a copied bind with another `feeTo` fails `BadLinkSignature`. `feeTo` cannot be zero or the drop itself (`BadFeeTo`) |
| F2 / F4 outside approvals | ERC-1271 and PqGuard approvals signed the same untagged message format as Pocket intents | ERC-1271 signs `qorechain-msg-v1\|qorechain-diana\|pocket-erc1271-v1:0x<digest>` over `keccak256(POCKET_ERC1271_V1, chainid, pocket, hash)`; PqGuard signs `...\|pqguard-v1:0x<digest>` over `keccak256(PQGUARD_APPROVAL_V1, chainid, account, digest)`. An outside approval is never a valid Pocket intent, and it is bound to one account on one chain |
| F3 router value | `PocketRouter` had `receive()` and swept its balance to the caller | no `receive()`, no sweep: the router cannot hold QOR |
| Stage 4 cleanups | (prepared in `docs/audit/stage4/STAGE4_DIFF.md`) | `onlyApprovedThresholdRecovery` / `onlyApprovedUnanimousRecovery` modifiers, subtractive drip headroom, the `guardianFreeze` assert, `len > MAX_GUARDIANS` fail-fast with loop bounds |
| NatSpec | partial | every contract, function, event and error |

Protocol messages (owner intents, guardian approvals, link and claim signatures) keep the v4/v5 format, so the app and
QoreX sign them as before. Only the bind (F1) and outside approvals (F2) changed what is signed. The relayer API accepts a
v5.1 bind only when `feeTo` is the relayer (`backend/src/route.ts`, `bindFeeToIssue`); a user who relays it themselves
signs their own address as `feeTo`.
